## Purpose

A single-page portfolio for recruiters and potential clients to understand Lenard's capabilities quickly and inspect his work in depth.
The first impression should communicate discipline, a concrete contribution, and direct resume/contact access.
The ten-second target is a design objective to be checked with people, not an established claim about recruiter behavior.

## Approved direction

White, extremely minimal, information-focused, responsive, and grounded in real qualifications and builds.
Sequence: introduction with capability summary and contact/social links; selected builds; background and leadership; education and credentials; closing contact.
Credentials use automatic slides with full manual access and a parallel readable list.
The carousel is the only automatic motion.
Original documents may open in Drive when public links exist; local originals allow completion without Drive.
